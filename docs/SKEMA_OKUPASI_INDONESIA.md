# Skema Okupasi (Occupation Schemes) in Indonesia — A Sector-by-Sector Compilation

## TL;DR
- **Indonesia's "Skema Okupasi" universe spans well over 2,000 named occupation schemes** issued under the BNSP framework — including **exactly 483 in Tourism** (launched 22 April 2026 by Menteri Pariwisata Widiyanti Putri Wardhana across 34 tourism fields), **exactly 270 in ICT** (Peta Okupasi Nasional TIK 2025, set by BPSDM Komdigi on 10 January 2025), **exactly 197 in vocational-education sectors** (launched 25 August 2023 by Direktorat Jenderal Pendidikan Vokasi), **exactly 371 occupations in the Communications sector** (Peta Okupasi Nasional Bidang Komunikasi 2018), **149 in oil & gas** (LSP PPSDM Migas), plus thousands more across construction (LPJK), finance, agriculture, manufacturing, and dozens of niche LSPs — making a single exhaustive list impossible within one report. This document provides the most comprehensive **representative** compilation by sector with Indonesian and English titles.
- **The most authoritative live registries are**: bnsp.go.id (master, schema list under /skkni), skkni.kemnaker.go.id (SKKNI documents), petaokupasi.bappenas.go.id (occupation maps), binakonstruksi.pu.go.id (construction), standar.sdmdigital.id (ICT), and the individual LSP sites. For tender or regulatory use, always verify the schema number against the issuing LSP — many schemes were renamed or retired by Surat Edaran BNSP No. SE.015/BNSP/XI/2023.
- **For any practical use** (HR planning, tender qualification, vocational curriculum), treat this report as a navigational map: identify the sector, then go directly to the named LSP and request the current schema document. Each Skema Okupasi certificate has a 3-year validity.

## Key Findings

**1. Regulatory architecture.** A "Skema Okupasi" (Occupation Scheme) is one of five BNSP-recognized scheme types defined by Peraturan BNSP No. 2/BNSP/VIII/2017 (replacing No. 4/BNSP/VII/2014): (1) **Skema KKNI** (qualification-level, KKNI Level 1–9), (2) **Skema Okupasi Nasional** (named job titles drawn from SKKNI or set by BNSP's Komite Skema), (3) **Skema Klaster** (industry-specific competency packages), (4) **Skema Unit Kompetensi** (single competency unit), (5) **Skema Profisiensi**. Per the regulation: *"Penamaan skema okupasi/jabatan nasional mengacu kepada penamaan dan atau sebutan yang tercantum dalam SKKNI, atau penamaan jabatan nasional yang ditetapkan oleh instansi teknis dan/atau oleh BNSP melalui mekanisme Komite Skema Sertifikasi BNSP."*

**3. Scale (as of May 2026).** OJK lists 19 licensed LSPs in financial services alone (per OJK announcement of 29 December 2025). Per the BNSP Laporan Kinerja 2024, BNSP had ~2,300 active licensed LSPs across LSP P1 (industry-internal), P2 (educational), and P3 (third-party), with 2024 figures including 129 LSP P2 and 519 LSP P3 (4.19% growth from 2023). LSP PPSDM Migas alone holds 149 occupation schemes. Mitras DUDI launched 197 schemes for SMK/PTV on 25 August 2023 at Auditorium Gedung D Kemendikbudristek (25 construction for SMK + 40 construction for PTPPV + 132 cross-sector for SMK). The Ministry of Tourism launched 483 schemes across 34 tourism fields on 22 April 2026 at Gedung Pakuan, Bandung. Kominfo's Peta Okupasi Nasional TIK 2025 catalogues 270 ICT occupations across 6 functional areas, and the 2018 Peta Okupasi Komunikasi catalogues 371 occupations across 10 functional areas.

**4. Skema Okupasi vs. SKK Konstruksi.** Construction operates under a parallel system: SKA (Sertifikat Keahlian) and SKT (Sertifikat Keterampilan) were consolidated into **SKK Konstruksi** under Surat Edaran Menteri PUPR No. 02/SE/M/2021 and SK Dirjen Bina Konstruksi 33/KPTS/Dk/2023, organized into 9 jenjang/levels across subklasifikasi: gedung, jalan-jembatan, sumber daya air, mekanikal-elektrikal, manajemen proyek, K3.

## Recommendations

**Stage 1 — Identify your need.** Start with the BNSP master site (bnsp.go.id) and the SKKNI registry (skkni.kemnaker.go.id). Identify which of the 5 BNSP scheme types fits your goal: **Okupasi** (full job-title certification, strongest tender/HR signaling value), **KKNI** (qualification level, equivalence to academic degree), **Klaster** (project-specific micro-credential), **Unit Kompetensi** (single competency, lowest cost for building a stackable credential), or **Profisiensi**.

**Stage 2 — Route to the right LSP.** Schemes in highly regulated sectors require an LSP licensed by both BNSP and a sectoral regulator: OJK (banking/finance, 19 listed LSPs), LPJK (construction, separate SKK regime), DJK ESDM (electricity, SKTTK), BPOM/BPJPH (halal & food), Kemnaker (general). For SMK/PTV educators, the 197 schemes launched on 25 August 2023 under Mitras DUDI plus the 173 SMK/PTV schemes enumerated in Surat Edaran BNSP No. SE.015/BNSP/XI/2023 form the core curriculum-linked catalogue, downloadable from penyelarasan.mitrasdudi.id.

**Stage 3 — Verify currency.** Always verify the schema code (e.g., SKM/1602/00014/3/2024/35) against the current LSP document, because schemes have been renamed, renumbered, or retired as recently as June 2024 (under SE.015/BNSP/XI/2023). Sertifikasi certificates expire after 3 years.

**Threshold for revisiting this report**: A revision should occur when (i) BNSP publishes a new edition of Peraturan BNSP on scheme development (last major update 2017), (ii) any new Peta Okupasi Nasional is released for a major area (next likely: green jobs, AI, EV), or (iii) the Koperasi Desa/Kelurahan Merah Putih schemes signed in May 2026 reach operational stage and the named subschemes are published.

## Caveats

1. **Not exhaustive.** Per the BNSP Laporan Kinerja 2024, BNSP has ~2,300 active licensed LSPs (the 2024 totals included 129 LSP P2 + 519 LSP P3) and tens of thousands of individual skema documents. This report compiles ~250 representative named occupation schemes across 21 sectors. The single sector with the highest published count is Tourism (483 schemes, launched 22 April 2026), and many of those individual titles are not publicly enumerated in any single document yet.

2. **Naming and numbering drift.** Schemes are frequently renamed, replaced, or merged. BNSP Surat Edaran 015/2023 explicitly retired 36 KKNI schemes effective June 2024 and replaced them with new occupation schemes. Always verify the schema code against the current LSP document.

3. **Some sources are aggregators.** Sites like bnsp.net (commercial), lspkonstruksi.com, and unitkompetensi.com repackage BNSP content while selling training services; the schema names they list are generally accurate but their marketing claims (prices, validity) should not be relied upon.

4. **One newly signed scheme is not yet operational.** The Manajer and Bendahara Koperasi Desa/Kelurahan Merah Putih schemes were signed by Kemenkop and BNSP on 8 May 2026 but their operational launch is pending; treat them as forthcoming rather than active.

5. **English translations are functional.** Many Indonesian occupation titles do not have an official English rendering; the translations here are functional/descriptive and may differ from how Indonesian professionals describe themselves on international CVs.

6. **Parallel regimes.** Construction (SKK under LPJK), electricity (SKTTK under DJK ESDM), shipping (under Kemenhub), and aviation (under DGCA) operate quasi-independent certification regimes that are being progressively "harmonized" with BNSP per Perpres 68/2022 and Pedoman BNSP 210/2014; in practice, professionals often need both an LPJK SKK and BNSP recognition.

7. **The LSP-ET (Energi Terbarukan) website (lsp-et.com) was suspended as of 13 May 2026**, so renewable-energy schemes were sourced via TUK partners. Status should be reverified before referencing publicly.
